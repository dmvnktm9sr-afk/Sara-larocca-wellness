What is Biofield Tuning?

Listening to the field around you, with sound.

Biofield Tuning is a sound therapy method that uses tuning forks to explore the biofield — the field of energy that surrounds and moves through your body — and to bring it back into a clearer, steadier tone.

Early life The years between Now

Your field holds your story

In the Biofield Tuning model, the field reaches roughly one and a half metres out from the body, and our experiences leave traces in it — a little like rings in a tree. The present sits close to the body; early life lies out near the edge.

Listening with the forks

I strike a fork at the outer edge of your field and move slowly towards you, listening. Where the tone changes — fuzzy, flat or uneven — I stay, holding the fork there until the sound clears and steadies. Weighted forks placed on the body let you feel the vibration too.

Where it comes from

The method was developed by researcher and practitioner Eileen Day McKusick, who began working with sound on the body in 1996. Using tuning forks in her massage practice, she noticed their tone changed in the space around her clients. Today it’s taught to practitioners all over the world.

It’s simple, gentle and non-invasive — no needles, no massage. You rest; the sound does the work.

What a session is like

Slow, from start to finish.

Before

Arrive and land

Come in, take your shoes off, have some water. We start with a short, easy conversation about what brings you here and how you’d like to feel when you leave.

During

Rest on the table

You lie fully clothed under a blanket while I work with the forks in the field around you and on the body. Some people drift off, some notice warmth or tingling, some just enjoy the quiet.

After

Go gently

We take a few minutes to talk about the session. Then drink plenty of water, rest if you can, and spend a little time outside — barefoot on the grass if the weather allows.

Before

A short call

We meet by phone, video or WhatsApp to talk about what brings you here and how you’d like to feel. Have a quiet, warm spot ready where you can lie down undisturbed.

During

Rest at home

You lie down under a blanket and rest while I work with the forks here in Sjuntorp. Phone on silent, eyes closed — there’s nothing you need to do.

After

Reconnect

We speak again to share what each of us noticed. Then stay cosy, drink water and keep the rest of the day slow if you can.

Good to know

How many sessions should I book?+

A single session is a lovely way to try it. Many people choose to begin with three, about a week apart, since each session builds on the one before. After that, some come back with the seasons.

What might I feel?+

Everyone is different. Some feel warmth, tingling or a wave of emotion; some fall asleep; some notice very little in the moment and more in the days after. All of it is completely normal.

How should I prepare?+

Wear something comfortable and drink plenty of water during the day. If you can, leave a little space in your calendar afterwards rather than rushing straight on to the next thing.

How does a distance session work?+

Distance sessions follow the same process as in person — I simply work with the forks from my room in Sjuntorp while you rest at home. You can book from anywhere in the world; the booking page shows times in your own time zone.
